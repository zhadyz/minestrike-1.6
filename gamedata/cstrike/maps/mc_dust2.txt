Dust II - Minecraft Edition
de_dust2 rebuilt from 123 x 141 x 37 Minecraft blocks.

Everything here is a block: mine it with a pickaxe,
build cover, blow craters with TNT and HE grenades,
open and close the doors.

Counter-Terrorists: stop the Terrorists from bombing
the A and B sites.
Terrorists: plant the C4 at A or B.

Press B for the creative inventory: diamond and
netherite gear, elytra, fireworks, tools and blocks.
Watch out for the Creeper.
